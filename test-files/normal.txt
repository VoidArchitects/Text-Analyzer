Text analysis is a fundamental process in software engineering and natural language processing. It involves examining textual data to extract meaningful metrics, patterns, and insights.

Common text processing tasks include counting words, lines, and characters, as well as finding frequency distributions and searching for specific key terms. A reliable analyzer should handle regular prose smoothly and produce accurate results.
